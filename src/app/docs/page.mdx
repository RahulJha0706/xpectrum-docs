# Introduction

Welcome to the Xpectrum documentation. Xpectrum is the workspace for building, deploying and monitoring AI agents: you design an agent on a visual canvas, ground it in your own knowledge, connect the tools it needs, and put it on a phone number, SMS, WhatsApp, email, your website or your own app through the API.

## What you can build

- **Voice agents** that answer your phone line, book appointments and transfer calls — or call a list of numbers for you.
- **Chat and support agents** that answer on your website, WhatsApp, SMS and email from your own documents.
- **Automations** that run on a schedule or a webhook: fetch data, reason over it with a model, and write results back to your systems.
- **Client workspaces** if you are an agency building agents for other businesses.
