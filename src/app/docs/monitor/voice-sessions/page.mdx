# Voice sessions

**Monitoring & Analytics › Sessions** lists every voice call an agent handled, whether over a phone number (inbound or outbound) or in the browser, with charts for volume and pickup rate, an outcome for each call, and a detail page with an AI summary, the recording and the transcript.

Everyone who can build apps can open **Sessions**. Pick an agent in the sidebar first; until you do, the page shows **Select an agent to view sessions**.

## Filters and time range

| Control | Options |
|---|---|
| Time range | **Past 1 hour**, **Past 24 hours** (default), **Past 7 days**, **Past 30 days**, or **Custom date** |
| Status (table) | **All Status**, **Active**, **Closed**, **Error** |
| **Refresh** | Reload the charts and the table |

Times display in your account's time zone if you've set one other than UTC, otherwise in your browser's.

## Summary tiles

| Tile | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Total Calls** | Calls in the selected range. |
| **Avg Duration** | Average call length, with the total below. |
| **Success Rate** | Share of calls that were answered and had a conversation. |
| **Calls Today** | Calls today, and how many are active now. |

## Charts

| Chart | What it shows | Use it to |
|---|---|---|
| **Inbound vs Outbound** | Calls split into **Inbound** (phone calls to your numbers), **Outbound** (calls the agent placed) and **WebRTC** (in-browser calls, such as the web voice widget or the **Call** button). | See where your call volume comes from. |
| **Peak Concurrent Calls** | How many calls were active at the same time over the range, with the **Peak** and **Avg**. | Plan for busy periods. |
| **Call Volume Over Time** | Total calls per interval. | Spot busy hours and days. |
| **Outbound Pickup Rate** | Outbound calls **Picked Up** versus **Missed**. | Judge a campaign's reach and best calling times. |

## Sessions table

| Column | Shows |
|---|---|
| **Session ID** | Short session ID; click to open the detail. |
| **Room Name** | The call's room identifier. |
| **Started At**, **Ended At**, **Duration** | Timing. |
| **Participants** | Number of participants (caller, agent, and any others). |
| **Features** | **Agents** when an agent joined; **SIP** for phone calls. |
| **From**, **To** | Phone numbers for phone calls. |
| **Outcome** | The call outcome (see below), with ↗ for outbound and ↙ for inbound. |
| **Sentiment** | Positive, neutral or negative, from the AI summary. |
| **Status** | **ACTIVE** (call in progress), **CLOSED** or **ERROR**. |

The table shows 20 calls per page.

### Outcome badges

| Badge | Meaning | Typical next step |
|---|---|---|
| **TALKED** | The call was answered and a conversation took place. | Read the summary; check it was resolved. |
| **NO TALK** | The call was answered but no conversation happened. | Replay it: the greeting may not have played, the caller may have hung up at once, or speech wasn't picked up. |
| **NO ANSWER** | Nobody answered. | For campaigns, try again at another time. |
| **REJECTED** | The call was declined. | Don't redial straight away. |
| **FAILED** | The call couldn't be connected. | Check the number, its format and the caller number's setup. |
| **VOICEMAIL** | The call reached voicemail. | Follow up by SMS. |

<Callout>
  The outcome describes whether the call connected. How the conversation went is in the **AI Summary**, which has its own outcome (**Resolved**, **Unresolved**, **Escalated**, **No contact**).
</Callout>

## Session detail

Click a **Session ID** to open the detail. **Back to Sessions** returns to the list.

| Section | Contents |
|---|---|
| Info cards | **Room Name**, **Room Session ID**, **Unique Participants**, **Start Time**, **End Time**, **Duration**, **Agent App** |
| **AI Summary** | Generated from the call transcript: a sentiment (**Positive**, **Neutral**, **Negative**), an outcome (**Resolved**, **Unresolved**, **Escalated**, **No contact**), a short summary, **Topics** and **Action items**. |
| **Recording** | Player for the call recording, with a download button. Expand it to see a two-channel waveform (**Agent** and **User**) and a synced transcript. |
| **Transcript** | Each turn with a timestamp, agent and caller. Shown on its own when there's no recording. |
| **Session Timeline** | One bar per participant from when they joined to when they left; **Ongoing** for a call in progress. |
| **Publishers** | Participants with **Participant Identity**, **Name**, **Type** (**Agent**, **SIP**, **Egress**, **User**), **Joined At**, **Left At**. |
| **Events** | Room events such as **Room Started** and **Participant Joined**, with an **Event payload** button for the raw JSON. |

### AI Summary states

| Message | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Analyzing this call…** | The summary is being generated. Refresh in a moment. |
| **No transcript available to analyze.** | Nothing was said, so there's nothing to summarize. |
| **Analysis failed — …** | The summary couldn't be generated; the reason follows. |
| **No analysis yet.** | The call hasn't been analyzed. |

## Recordings

Recordings exist only for calls where **Record Session** was on in the agent's voice **Configurations** (it's off by default). See [Voice](/docs/channels/voice).

- Click **Audio** to download the recording as an `.ogg` file. The agent is on one channel and the caller on the other.
- Click the waveform to jump to a point in the call, or click a transcript line (**Jump to this moment**) to play from there.

<Callout type='warning'>
  Recording laws differ by country and state. If you turn on **Record Session**, tell callers they're being recorded where the law requires it, for example in your **Initial Message**.
</Callout>

## Troubleshooting

- **No sessions listed** → pick the agent in the sidebar, widen the time range, and check the status filter isn't set to **Active** or **Error**.
- **No Recording section** → **Record Session** was off when the call happened. Turn it on and click **Publish Changes** on the Voice page; earlier calls can't be recorded retroactively.
- **Transcript is missing the greeting** → a greeting spoken before the caller fully joined may not appear in the transcript.
- **Many NO TALK outcomes** → replay a few. Check the **Initial Message** and **Initial Message Delay (seconds)**, and that the transcriber **Language** matches your callers.
- **Many calls end at the same length** → they're hitting **Call Duration**. Raise it in the voice **Configurations**.
- **Sessions show ERROR** → the call failed to start or dropped. Check the agent is published, has a model, and that the workspace has credit. See [Voice troubleshooting](/docs/troubleshooting/voice).
